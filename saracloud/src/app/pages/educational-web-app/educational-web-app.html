01 — ABOUT EDUSAFE

EduSafe is a responsive web-based school management platform developed as part of the Tech4Dev Capstone Project. The application focuses on creating a modern, user-friendly and secure learning environment by providing authentication, centralized access to information, notifications and AI-powered assistance.

PROJECT-TITLE EduSafe
TYPE Educational Web Application
PROGRAM Women Techsters Bootcamp, Cohort 5.1
PROJECT TYPE Team Project
02 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

One platform for a connected learning experience.

EduSafe brings students, teachers, and parents together through role-based dashboards designed around their different educational needs. Each user can access relevant information, tools, and activities from a centralized platform.

01

Student Dashboard

Students can access their courses, monitor attendance and grades, take online quizzes, view upcoming activities, and use the AI-powered study assistant for learning support.

Courses Attendance Grades & GPA Quizzes AI Study Buddy
02

Teacher Dashboard

Teachers can manage classes, access student information, create and grade assignments, organize course materials, and share educational resources.

Class Management Student Roster Assignments Grading Resources
03

Parent Dashboard

Parents can monitor their children's academic progress, check attendance and grades, view school announcements, and access quick actions for staying connected with teachers and school activities.

Student Selection Attendance Grades Announcements Teacher Contact

03 — PROBLEM STATEMENT

What problem were we trying to solve?

Many schools face challenges in managing student information, maintaining secure access, communicating effectively, and providing easy access to academic resources.

01

Student Information Management

Schools need a centralized way to manage and access important student information efficiently.

02

Secure Authentication

Users need secure and reliable authentication to protect access to their accounts and educational information.

03

Effective Communication

Students, teachers, and parents need better ways to communicate and stay informed about important academic activities.

04

Access to Academic Resources

Learners and educators need convenient access to relevant academic information and learning resources.

04 — THE SOLUTION

A more connected learning experience.

EduSafe was designed to address these challenges by bringing essential school information, communication, learning support, and secure access together in one responsive platform.

01

Secure Authentication

Provide users with secure authentication and account access through registration, login, and email verification.

02

Better Communication

Improve communication between students, teachers, and parents through notifications and accessible school information.

03

Centralized Information

Bring important academic and school information together in one centralized platform for easier access and management.

04

AI-Powered Assistance

Provide learners with an AI-powered study assistant to support their learning experience and access to academic help.

05

Responsive Experience

Deliver a consistent and accessible experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices through responsive design.

THE GOAL

Making education more accessible, connected, and organized.

05 — KEY FEATURES

What EduSafe offers.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Leading the team,
building EduSafe together.

As the Team Lead, I coordinated the team throughout the development of EduSafe, ensuring that everyone stayed aligned and contributed towards the same goal. We worked closely and collaboratively from the planning stage through to the final product.

The Product Managers guided the project through the PRD and defined the product requirements, while the Product Designer provided the UI/UX designs that shaped the web application's interface. Working alongside my fellow Software Developers, we translated these requirements and designs into a functional web application, implementing the features, reviewing our work, solving challenges, and refining the product together.

My role was to coordinate these efforts, facilitate communication, keep the team on track, and contribute to the development process while ensuring that our collective work came together as one cohesive product.
